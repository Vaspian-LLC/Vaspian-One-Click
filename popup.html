    Help & diagnostics

    Make a call

    Type a number or extension on Calls and press Enter. Your phone rings first, then connects. On a web page, click a phone number, or select one and press Alt+Shift+C.

    During a call

    The call card shows who you're talking to. Hold, transfer, park or hang up from there. On Team and Settings, a small bar keeps the call in view.

    Team

    See who's free, call or transfer to anyone, join or leave your ring groups, and pick up parked calls. Turn Do not disturb on or off at the top of the panel.

    Recent calls

    Your last 500 calls, filtered by All, Missed, Inbound or Outbound. Select a call to add a note, or its phone button to call back. Download saves a CSV. Signing out clears your history.

    Screen pop and wrap-up

    Screen pop opens the caller in your CRM when a call rings or when you answer. Wrap-up asks how an answered call went and saves your choice and note with the call.
